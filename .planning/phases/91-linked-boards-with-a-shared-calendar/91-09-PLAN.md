<objective>
Show the linked boards' dates where clashes are actually decided: the Quest Details date-vote calendar (D-12). Desktop gets linked chips in the grid at all three `_Calendar` call sites, plus the "Also showing … (read-only)" note. Mobile gets an "Also that day: Board B — Title, time" line under each clashing proposed date. Linked entries never reach the vote markup. Then pin the whole read-only contract with a static and reflection guard.

Purpose: the DM proposing dates, and the players voting on them, must see the other boards' sessions while choosing. This is the surface the roadmap calls out as the one most likely to prevent a double booking.
Output: QuestController.Details linked read, the `_LinkedAlsoThatDay.cshtml` partial and its slot, quests.mobile.css rules, the Details integration suite and the static guard.
</objective>
