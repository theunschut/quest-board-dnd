  <what-built>
Phase 91 in full:
- **Platform.** A Linked Boards page under the SuperAdminOnly policy, on desktop and mobile. It stores a named set of same-type boards as a nullable `Groups.LinkedBoardSetId`, created by the `AddLinkedBoardSets` migration.
- **Linked read.** For a board in a set, `/calendar` and the Quest Details date-vote calendar (desktop and mobile) also show the other boards' quests and events. The read is read-only, month-windowed, pinned to the linked board ids and re-checked. Entries link for members and are plain text for everyone else.
- **DM-only rule.** DM-only sessions now follow the viewer's role on the owning board, on own and linked calendars alike.

The automated suites run on EF Core InMemory, which cannot prove two things. First, that the migration applies on SQL Server. Second, that the windowed linked queries translate, because InMemory evaluates them as plain LINQ. Those, and the visual backstops, need you.
  </what-built>
