<objective>
Make the three nightly sweeps fire at the hour their registration claims, and make the fallback state visible from outside the container.

Purpose: correcting the cron without correcting `DailyReminderJob`'s own notion of "today" — or the reverse — leaves the sweep computing on a different clock than the one that woke it, which the ROADMAP names as a worse state than the one this phase starts from. Both halves ship in this one plan so they cannot diverge.

Output: `RecurringJobOptionsFactory.ForBoardZone`, three corrected Hangfire registrations, `BoardTimeZoneHealthCheck`, a board-clock-driven `DailyReminderJob`, and four corrected comments.
</objective>
