<objective>
Convert the QuestLog, Contacts and Platform-Group real-instant render sites to `Html.LocalTime`, and split the four coalesce sites that currently push a wall-clock value and a real instant through one format string.

Purpose: the coalesce is the phase's sharpest landmine — `FinalizedDate` is what the calendar feed emits as floating local time, and a blanket conversion over these lines would move every subscriber's session by the board's offset. Splitting it is the only way to handle either side correctly.

Output: eight converted view files (four desktop, four mobile twins), with every wall-clock branch provably untouched.
</objective>

Two resolutions this plan makes visible rather than applying silently.

**A. `Areas/Platform/Views/Group/Index.cshtml:51` is in scope (RESEARCH.md over CONTEXT.md, correction 3).** `item.CreatedAt` is a real instant rendered raw as `"yyyy-MM-dd"` — the one raw-ISO outlier in the whole view tree. Its mobile twin `Index.Mobile.cshtml:42` renders the same property and is converted in the same task per the parity rule.

**B. Planner resolution — the `FinalizedDate ?? ClosedDate` coalesce.** Four sites (`QuestLog/Details.cshtml:37`, `Details.Mobile.cshtml:38`, `Index.cshtml:23`, `Index.Mobile.cshtml:50`) render `(quest.FinalizedDate ?? quest.ClosedDate)` through one `.ToString(...)` call. `86-UI-SPEC.md` §3's call-site table does not list them; `86-RESEARCH.md` files them under "Wall-clock — never convert" with a **Landmine** flag, and its Pitfall 1 directs: *"Split into two branches — render `FinalizedDate` untouched when present, and only pass through `Html.LocalTime(...)` for the `ClosedDate` fallback branch."* They are covered here rather than dropped. `ClosedDate` is unambiguously a real instant (assigned `DateTime.UtcNow` at `QuestBoard.Repository/QuestRepository.cs:170`) and CONTEXT.md's classification table lists it on the real-instant side.

Style assignment uses UI-SPEC §3's own escape clause for newly identified real-instant sites — *"it must pick one of the four styles above rather than inventing a fifth"* — matched to each site's current granularity: `date-time` on the two Details views (currently a date-and-time format) and `date` on the two Index views (currently date-only).

**The cost this accepts, stated plainly:** in a completed-quest list, a quest that was finalized and a quest that was only closed will now render their "Completed" date in two different format strings — the finalized one keeps its existing wording verbatim, the closed one takes the canonical style. That asymmetry is the price of the classification being correct, and it is a copy difference, not a time difference. It is flagged for the human-verification checkpoint in 86-06.
</recorded_correction>
