| Return and Sell from the purchase history fail on an item with limited stock | `ShopService.ReturnOrSellItemAsync` through `BaseRepository.UpdateAsync` and `EntityProfile` | Root cause: the service loads the purchase and the buyer's history before it reads the item, so EF's fix-up puts those tracked purchases into the tracked item's `Transactions`, and `UpdateAsync` then mapped the domain item over the tracked entity navigations included, replacing that collection with untracked copies. Fix: the `ShopItem -> ShopItemEntity` map ignores `CreatedByDm`, `Group` and `Transactions` and the `UserTransaction -> UserTransactionEntity` map ignores `User`, `ShopItem` and `OriginalTransaction`, so a mapped domain model writes only its own columns and foreign keys; every write still goes through the tracked entity and SaveChanges (no raw SQL, bulk write, Attach or hand-set state). The same fix covers Purchase, Sell to Shop and the management actions, which share the pattern. The four reproduction cases of `HistorySlipForm_SubmittedAsRendered_RefundsTheRemainingQuantityAndRestocks` are un-skipped and green. Fix commit `7a323074` (plan 99-23). Escalated by the validation audit; fixed on the operator's decision "Fix + 2 gaps". |
| Archive accepts any status, and Reopen publishes any archived item | `ShopManagementController.Archive` and `Reopen` | Archive now accepts only a Published item, for every role (Dungeon Master and Admin alike); a Draft, Denied or Archived item is refused with "Only published items can be archived." and keeps its status, so Archive then Reopen cannot publish a Draft or Denied item without another Dungeon Master's review. Reopen and the management lists are unchanged (the lists already offered Archive only on Published rows and Reopen only on Archived rows; a render fact now pins it). Fix commits `b6c80468` (list, Purchase and Sell to Shop) and `fd84daad` (Details) (plan 99-23), on the operator's decision "Fix + 2 gaps". |
