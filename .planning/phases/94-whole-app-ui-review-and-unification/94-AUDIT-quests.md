# Phase 94 Audit: Quests and Quest Log area (`A-quests`)

Audit of the Quests area on desktop and mobile, done in senior-frontend Critic mode against the frozen baseline (port 8001, commit `b6770322`). Deliverable 1 for the area; it feeds the index file, the operator questions at direction sign-off and the Quests area phase.

## Scope

- **Views audited (11 pairs, every one with a `.Mobile.cshtml` twin):** `Views/Quest/{Index,Details,Create,Edit,CreateFollowUp,Manage}`, `Views/QuestLog/{Index,Details,EditRecap}`, `Views/Home/Index`, `Views/Move/Index`. Quest Details is audited in two states (finalized, and voting open).
- **Partials audited through the pages that render them (tagged shared partial):** `Quest/_QuestFormScripts`, `Shared/_MarkdownEditor`; `_QuestCard` and `_QuestSection` are unreferenced (dead-code). `Shared/_Calendar` and `Shared/_CharacterSelectModal` render inside Quest Details but belong to area 2 and area 3; findings that live inside them are tagged and cross-referenced.
- **Stylesheets:** the 11 area sheets plus `dm-create.mobile.css` and `dm-manage.mobile.css`, which only `Quest/Create.Mobile` and `Quest/Manage.Mobile` load (see Area-phase scope notes).
- **Evidence base:** the BEFORE record of plan 94-02 (desktop 1440 and Pixel 7 mobile full-page captures, `before/report.json`), re-read view files and stylesheets, and scripted extractions kept under `$QBR/audit/quests/` (`inv.mjs` twin inventories, `cssmetrics.mjs`, `cssgrep.mjs`, `deadclasses.mjs`, `markup.mjs`, `cf.mjs` contrast grouping, `targets.mjs`, `measure.mjs`). `$QBR` is `~/qb-ui-review/phase-94`. Screenshots are never committed; they are referenced by scratch path only, for example `~/qb-ui-review/phase-94/before/desktop/Quest-Details.png`.
- **Excluded from the area counts:** the shell (nav bar text 3.78:1 at the right-hand gradient end, the unnamed nav toggler and GitHub footer link, the footer and brand questions). Those are foundation items; the probe lists 7 shell failures per desktop capture and 1 per mobile capture, and they are not repeated here.
- **Fixtures:** only the fantasy-named fixtures on the Audit Board; no member name, email or user-entered text from real data is quoted. Findings cite selectors, classes, ratios and `file:line`.
- **Severity key:** S1 text hidden, unreadable or a control unreachable; S2 visibly wrong or fails the AA bar; S3 inconsistency with the system; S4 polish. Contrast is measured against D-16 (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large and non-text, composited over B-max `#794317`) with `contrast.mjs`.
- **Area phase column:** `1` is the Quests and Quest Log phase of the draft cut.
