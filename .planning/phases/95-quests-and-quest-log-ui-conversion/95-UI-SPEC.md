**Content stack and height budget (14px floor; line heights 1.5 body, 1.2 title; gap 8).** DM line 21px; date block 42px (two lines); count line 21px; CR badge 28px. Open quest 94px plus title; finalized quest 144px plus title; Campaign board: DM line only (21 + 8). A finalized quest with a one-line 20px title needs 176px, with two lines 200px.

**Poster choice (D-05).** Stable per quest, derived from the quest id with an integer mix (`(uint)id * 2654435761u`), never `string.GetHashCode()` (randomised per process). The pick is made among the posters whose box holds the estimated content; if none does, `p1` at title tier 2. The same pick chooses the wax seal design from the four `Wax Seals` images and is stable per quest. Today's character-count thresholds are removed.
**Seal-aware budget (planner rule, verified at Gate 1):** where a seal slot lies under the box (`p1` bottom-left), the budget is cut by the seal's overlap (about 40px on `p1`) so text never runs under a seal.

### Poster text treatment (D-08) — decided at Gate 1: heavier ink only

No lightened zone, no feather, no tint and no halo. The v1 zone (15 / 25 / 35% `color-mix` on `--qb-surface-paper`) was rejected by the operator as a visible block; the v2 halos (A tight, B soft) passed the probe only because the halo paints paper under each letter, and were not chosen. Text is `--qb-ink`; the meta lines (labels and values) are weight 600; titles keep the display face at the strong weight. The CR badge is unchanged.

Option C leaves poster text **below 4.5:1 per glyph** (worst-glyph 10th percentile, darkest pixel in brackets): `p1` 2.46 (1.50), `p2` 3.65 (2.81), `p4` 2.92 (2.52), `p5` 3.45 (2.09), `p6` 3.00 (2.55), `r1` 2.82 (1.69) — Ruined 1 over the burn mark is the weakest. This is a **recorded contrast exception** (Gate 1 item 2; `~/qb-ui-review/phase-95/contrast-exceptions.json`, kind `poster-glyph-floor`). It is held to a per-poster **floor** in the sweep probe's own measure: a later capture below its floor fails `readability-compare.py` and goes to the operator. The exception covers poster text on the desktop board only; every other surface keeps the 4.5:1 bar.

### Poster link, focus, hover (DISCRETION, from research Pattern 2)

- One real `<a class="row-nav-link" href="...">` per poster, the only class on the anchor (pinned by `RowNavigationAccessibilityTests`), inside the title heading; it goes to **Manage** for the quest's own DM and **Details** for everyone else, exactly as today. No `onclick`.
- Stretched in CSS: `.quest-poster .row-nav-link::after { content: ""; position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 1; }` on a `position: relative` poster. The foundation ring is applied to the poster with `:has(.row-nav-link:focus-visible)` (outline `--qb-focus-outer`, inner band `--qb-focus-inner`, widths and offset from `--qb-focus-width` / `--qb-focus-offset`), because the ring on the small link text alone would not show the whole target. The ring draws around the card's bounding box (it includes the poster's baked shadow margin); that is accepted. No `overflow: hidden` on a poster ancestor (foundation A-3).
- Hover: a lift of one `--qb-space-xs` (`translateY`) and a few degrees of seal tilt, **`transform` only**, `transition` on `transform` with `--qb-dur-base` / `--qb-ease`; no `filter` (the artwork already carries its shadow), no `transition: all`; nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Heading outline: today the board has no `h1` and the titles are `h3`. The contract adds `<h1 class="visually-hidden">Quest Board</h1>` (the page's existing `ViewData["Title"]`) on both layouts and makes poster titles `h2` (phone titles `h2`). **Planner assumption 3**: if the operator objects at Gate 1, the fallback is today's heading levels.
- Masonry: card heights come from CSS `aspect-ratio`, not `data-image-width/height`; `quest-board.js` positions cards with the CSSOM only and the page keeps a CSS column fallback so a script failure never hides the board. A `fantasy-quest-card` class is not carried over; the poster markup uses `quest-poster*` names.
