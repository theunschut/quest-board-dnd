the three foreign catch-alls are narrowed so they no longer reach inside the editor. Option (b): keep the tag;
`markdown-editor.css` stays unlayered with its two important-flagged rules, recorded as the one exception to the
"no important flag" point of the Definition of converted. Default if unanswered: (b).
