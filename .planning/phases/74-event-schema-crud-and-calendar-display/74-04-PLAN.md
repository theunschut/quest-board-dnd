<objective>
Add the request/response layer for events: the view model, its two AutoMapper entries, and the DM-gated CRUD controller that stamps the active board on every write and rejects a cross-board schedule reference before save.

Purpose: this is where the phase's highest-severity risk lives. `HasQueryFilter` constrains reads only — a mis-scoped group on an insert leaks across boards with no schema-level safety net, and this app has already shipped two real cross-tenant leaks. Every write path here derives the board server-side.
Output: `EventViewModel`, two profile entries, `EventsController` with six actions.
</objective>
