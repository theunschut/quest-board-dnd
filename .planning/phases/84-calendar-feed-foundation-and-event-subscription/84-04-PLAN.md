<objective>
Harden the proven feed path: make the date window configurable instead of hard-coded, make the retired-versus-unknown distinction real, throttle the fetch-time write so a polled address cannot become a write storm, rate limit the endpoint per address, and make it impossible for an address to reach a log line.

Purpose: the tracer proved the path works. These five controls are what make it safe to leave running unattended on the public internet with no reader watching it. Four of them are cheap; all four are the kind that get skipped and then matter.
Output: a configurable rolling window, the 410/404 split bounded by a retention sweep, a throttled timestamp write, a named rate-limiting policy, and logging that names a subscription by id only.
</objective>
