<objective>
Let a super admin move a one-off event to a linked board, from the same Move page. Answers from target members are kept as given and non-members' answers are dropped. A Campaign target fills in the automatic Yes rows a new Campaign event would have. Series occurrences and past events are refused, and nobody is emailed.

Purpose: D-08, D-09, D-12 and D-17's event half, on the shared contracts from plans 01 and 03 and the page from plan 04. Only one-off events can move, because the nightly series top-up would otherwise recreate a moved occurrence on its original date.

</objective>
