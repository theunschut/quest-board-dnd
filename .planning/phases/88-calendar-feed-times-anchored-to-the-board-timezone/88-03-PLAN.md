<objective>
Widen the tracer's proof from one default host to every zone a board can be configured with, over real anonymous HTTP: a non-default zone (Pacific/Auckland), an unresolvable zone that makes the clock fall back to UTC, and a Windows-style id — and lock the rule that the feed takes its zone only from the board clock into the existing source-level seam guard.

Purpose: D-06 accepts that a broken zone makes every subscriber's entries late while `/health` reports `Degraded`, but only if the feed declares exactly what the clock resolved. These facts prove that end to end, and that no configured string can leak into the document (CALTZ-03, CALTZ-05). The seam-guard extension adds files to a closed list and never removes one, so it strengthens `AmbientClockSeamTests` rather than weakening it.

Output: a new integration class with four live-feed facts and three additions to `AmbientClockSeamTests`.
</objective>
