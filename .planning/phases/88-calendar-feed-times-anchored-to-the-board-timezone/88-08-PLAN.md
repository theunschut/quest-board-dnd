<recorded_choices>
**Emit `LAST-MODIFIED`, equal to `DTSTAMP`, directly after it.**

RFC 5545 §3.8.7.2 says that in a calendar published with no method, `DTSTAMP` "is equivalent to the LAST-MODIFIED property". Clients are reported to key updates off one or the other, and no vendor documents which. Emitting both from one stored value costs one line per entry, cannot produce a disagreement, and gives a revision-comparing client the signal on whichever property it reads. `LAST-MODIFIED` is permitted at most once in a `VEVENT`, and nothing in the existing forbidden-property facts excludes it.
</recorded_choices>
