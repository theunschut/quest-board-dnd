<recorded_choices>
**A board rename is out of scope for this gap closure (operator decision).** Renaming a board does not re-signal existing calendar entries; each entry picks up the new board name on its next real revision. Raising every entry on a renamed board would need a new `IgnoreQueryFilters()` call site, and the operator dropped that path from v5.3.3. The limitation is recorded in CALTZ-12's text, in 88-05's recorded choice 4 and, through 88-09, in the architecture guidance and the validation notes.
</recorded_choices>
