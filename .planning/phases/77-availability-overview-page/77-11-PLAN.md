<objective>
Restore the mobile availability overview to the visual contract `77-UI-SPEC.md` line 70 assigns
it, and make its text pass WCAG AA.

The mobile card list currently paints itself with an entry-level treatment borrowed from the
mobile calendar: `.agenda-event-entry` is a small one-line row that lives *inside*
`.agenda-card-mobile`, a translucent glass wrapper. The availability overview copied the entry
but not the wrapper, so an entry treatment is doing page-level work directly against the
notice-board backdrop. The legend card on the same page uses the real glass surface, so the
page currently renders two design languages one above the other.

The knock-on defect is a measured contrast failure: the count block never sets a colour, so it
inherits dark body text that was correct on the desktop parchment table cell and became
near-invisible when the mobile card background went dark.

Purpose: EVTVIEW-01's mobile surface and EVTVIEW-03's "obvious at a glance" count are both
visually broken on a real device even though every automated assertion passes.
Output: a mobile card that is the glass surface, legible text at a stated contrast ratio, and
filled controls.

**Scope guard.** The card *shape* is not in question. D-17 (mobile is a separate user-agent
selected view rendering per-event cards, not the grid) and D-18 (each card leads with the counts,
with the per-member breakdown behind a tap) are locked and are unchanged by this plan — the
markup structure, the collapse mechanism and the card ordering all stay exactly as shipped. What
changes is the surface those cards are painted on, the colour of the text on it, and the fill of
two buttons. `77-UI-SPEC.md` is the authority for the target values here, not a thing being
renegotiated.
</objective>

<measured_contrast_reference>
Every ratio quoted in this plan was measured, not estimated, against the real backdrop. Reuse
these numbers rather than re-deriving them.

**Backdrop.** `body::before` in `mobile.css` paints `/images/Notice Board-blank2.jpg`
(3000x2000) with `background-size: cover; background-position: center`. On a 390x844 CSS-pixel
phone viewport that crops to the centre vertical strip `x=1038 y=0 w=924 h=2000`.

**Surface.** The glass is `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, so the effective background behind card
text is `0.15*255 + 0.85*backdrop` per channel.

| Quantity | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Glass composite over the visible crop — mean relative luminance | 0.0710 |
| Glass composite over the visible crop — worst (brightest) relative luminance | 0.1571 |
| Brightest backdrop pixel / its glass composite | rgb(128,73,26) → rgb(147,100,60) |
| Relative luminance of `#FFFFFF` | 1.0000 |
| Relative luminance of `#F4E4BC` | 0.7835 |

Contrast is `(L_lighter + 0.05) / (L_darker + 0.05)`.

| Foreground | vs worst composite | vs mean composite | WCAG floor that applies |
|------------|--------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| `#FFFFFF` | **5.07:1** | 8.68:1 | 4.5:1 (normal text under 18.66px bold) |
| `#F4E4BC` | 4.02:1 | 6.89:1 | 3.0:1 (large text — the 20px/600 card title) |

`#F4E4BC` is therefore fine for the 20px semibold card title (large text, 3:1 floor) but does
**not** clear the 4.5:1 normal-text floor at the brightest wood highlight. `#FFFFFF` clears the
normal-text floor across the entire visible backdrop. `#FFFFFF` is already part of this design
system (the vote chips, `.avail-cell-yes-muted`, `.modern-card .btn-primary`), so this
introduces no new colour token.

**Prior state for comparison:** the count block measured **1.34:1** — `rgb(33,37,41)` inherited
body text on the opaque `rgb(52,58,64)` card.
</measured_contrast_reference>

<design_decision>
The card is styled with the glass values written **directly into `.avail-card`**, rather than by
adding the `modern-card` class to the card element in the view. Both produce the identical
surface; the direct form is chosen because `modern-card.css` carries
`.modern-card span { color: #F4E4BC !important }`, which would cascade onto the vote chips
nested in each card's roster list and repaint their label text. The vote chips are explicitly
out of scope for this gap. Writing the five glass declarations into `.avail-card` reaches the
same visual result with no cascade reaching any descendant badge.
</design_decision>
