<objective>
Put the server gate on the remaining board pages named in D-02 (Shop management, Players, the DM profile, Characters, Contacts and contact categories), keep the picture endpoints open (D-07), and add a reflection test that refuses any unclassified action. After this plan, a typed URL or crafted POST to any switched-off page, or to Shop, Players or the DM profile on a Campaign board, redirects to the board's home with a toast, for every role.

Purpose: the server-side guard has to stand behind every hidden menu item (the project rule reinforced after Phase 52). Plan 01 proved the mechanism on Shop and plan 02 gated the quest, calendar, event and move controllers. This plan finishes the gate and makes omissions fail the build; it runs after plan 02 because the closed list needs every attribute in place and because it extends plan 02's test file.

Output: `[BoardPage]` and `[BoardPageExempt]` placed across 6 controllers, more facts in `BoardPageGateMatrixTests`, and `BoardPageCoverageTests`.
</objective>
