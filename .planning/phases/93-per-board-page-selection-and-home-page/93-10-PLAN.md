<recorded_choices>
1. **One flag, every consumer.** Making Quests switchable is a one-line policy change, because the Platform rows, the settings service, the gate, the menus, linked calendars and moves all read the policy. This plan adds only what is specific to quests: the live-quest refusal, the calendar condition and the DM profile history.
2. **The live rule is written once for SQL.** The count expresses `!IsClosed && !HasBeenPlayed(today)` as a translatable predicate, and a parity test pins it to `QuestExtensions.HasBeenPlayed` at the day boundary, so the two definitions cannot drift.
3. **An abandoned open quest blocks** (D-20): a OneShot quest still collecting votes with only past dates counts as live until a DM deletes it.
4. **DM profile history is the only extra view.** Every other quest link sits on a page that is itself gated, or is already inert (linked calendars), or is never loaded (the board's own calendar).
</recorded_choices>
