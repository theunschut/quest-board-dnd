<objective>
Bring the tracking documents into line with the code, now that both gaps blocking EVTRECUR-03 are
closed, and put the amendment to a prior phase's navigation decision on the record where a future

a Phase 37 decision. Phase 37's artifacts are archived and must stay that way, so without a record in
the live documents the next person to read NAV-01 will see shipped behaviour that contradicts it and
have no way to tell a deliberate amendment from a regression.

Purpose: leave the register truthful and the amendment discoverable.

`ROADMAP.md`.
