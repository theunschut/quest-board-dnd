<objective>
Close the second of the two gaps blocking EVTRECUR-03: a campaign board cannot reach the calendar
through normal navigation, and the calendar is where both of this phase's read surfaces live — the DM
horizon banner and the cancelled-occurrence chip. The open-ended campaign is precisely the case
EVTRECUR-03's own wording targets, so the requirement's user-visible guarantee fails for its primary
audience.

The same change also closes a live data wrinkle. `CalendarController` carries no board-type gate at
all — only the navigation entry is hidden — so the calendar is already reachable by address on a
campaign board today and already renders campaign quests. Excluding those quests is a fix to shipped
behaviour, not a precaution about future behaviour.

Purpose: make the rolling window observable where campaign DMs actually work, and make the campaign
calendar an events-only surface as the developer specified.
Output: a calendar navigation entry on campaign boards for both layouts, a board-type-aware quest load
in `CalendarController`, and the automated coverage locking all of it in.
</objective>

## Decision supersession — NAV-01 (Phase 37, milestone v6.0)

<!-- planner-discipline-allow: Nav_CampaignDm_CalendarLinkAbsent -->

This plan **supersedes the calendar clause of NAV-01**, the Phase 37 decision recorded in
`.planning/milestones/v6.0-phases/37-navigation-access-control/` and shipped in commit `f7a31fa9`.

- **What NAV-01 decided:** on a campaign board, the navigation is stripped down; the calendar entry,
  the shop entry, the manage-shop entry, the edit-my-profile entry and the players entry are all
  hidden. Phase 37's own threat register (`T-37-04`) recorded these as cosmetic only, explicitly
  noting the underlying controllers were never board-type gated and that gating them was out of scope.
- **What is superseded:** the calendar clause, and only the calendar clause. Phase 76 gives the
  calendar two campaign-relevant read surfaces that did not exist when NAV-01 was decided, which
  changes the premise the clause rested on.
- **What is NOT superseded and must stay green:** NAV-02 (shop absent), NAV-04 (manage shop absent),
  NAV-05 (edit my profile absent), NAV-06 (players absent), and the D-04 rule that a logged-out
  visitor sees no calendar entry. Their facts in `LayoutNavigationTests` are untouched by this plan.
- **The test that locks the superseded behaviour** is
  `LayoutNavigationTests.Nav_CampaignDm_CalendarLinkAbsent`, which asserts the rendered page does not
  contain the calendar label. It is **replaced**, not deleted, by
  `LayoutNavigationTests.Nav_CampaignDm_CalendarLinkPresent`, which asserts the new rule. A deletion
  would leave the amended clause unguarded and indistinguishable from an accidental regression.
- **What Phase 37 left cosmetic, this plan makes real for the calendar:** because the navigation entry
  is now exposed on campaign boards, the "nav hiding is not an access-control boundary" acceptance in
  `T-37-04` stops being adequate for this one route. The controller gains the board-type-aware quest
  load that Phase 37 declined to add.
- **Where the supersession is recorded:** plan `76-15` writes it into `.planning/ROADMAP.md` under
  Phase 76 and into `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` under the recurrence requirements section. The
  archived Phase 37 artifacts under `.planning/milestones/v6.0-phases/` are an immutable record and
  must not be edited by this plan or by 76-15.
