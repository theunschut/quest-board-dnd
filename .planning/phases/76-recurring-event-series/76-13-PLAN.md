<objective>
Close the first of the two gaps blocking EVTRECUR-03: the DM horizon banner renders on the desktop
calendar but has no counterpart on the mobile calendar, so a DM working from a phone gets no signal
at all when the rolling occurrence window stops advancing — reproducing exactly the silent failure
the banner exists to prevent (D-26).

Purpose: EVTRECUR-03's mechanism (materializer, nightly top-up job, filtered unique index) is correct
and independently re-verified. What fails is the requirement's user-facing half — a DM being able to
tell whether the rolling window is working. Half the surfaces where a DM looks currently answer that
question; the other half is blank.

This gap survived eight waves of green gates because no automated test asserts the banner renders on
either surface. A missing render breaks nothing. Task 1 therefore writes the assertion that would have
caught it, proves it fails, and only then is the markup ported.

Output: a mobile horizon banner matching the desktop copy contract, and the first automated coverage
of the banner on either surface.
</objective>
