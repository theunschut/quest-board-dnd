<objective>
Prove, through the real request pipeline with two genuinely distinct boards, that nothing this phase adds
can read or write across a board boundary — and that the two server-side refusals the phase depends on
hold when posted directly rather than merely being hidden in markup.

Purpose: the shipped integration harness runs with a single mutable group context defaulting to board 1,
which makes an ordinary test structurally incapable of catching a tenant leak. Every prior event phase
paid for this with a dedicated two-board class; this phase adds a background writer, a new controller and
four new mutating actions, so it needs its own.
Output: `EventSeriesTenantIsolationTests`.
</objective>
